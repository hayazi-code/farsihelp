# واژه‌چین: واژه‌نامهٔ گسترش‌یافته

Source: Saman Vaisipour, [Persian Lexicon](https://github.com/samanvp/persian-lexicon), MIT License (Copyright © 2022 Saman Vaisipour).

Source word lists: data/persian-words-4letter.txt through data/persian-words-10letter.txt. The upstream lists originate from the Uppsala Persian Corpus and include some names, rare terms, inflected forms, and nonwords. They are not an editorially verified word list.

Processing: normalize Arabic kaf/yeh to Persian kaf/yeh; strip surrounding whitespace; retain four-or-more-character strings consisting only of standard Persian letters; deduplicate; then select words using only a puzzle's seven characters and containing its mandatory center letter.

Imported unique dictionary candidates: 42,454.
Matches for five current boards: 239, 220, 120, 518, 269.

Words present in the game's earlier hand-curated list remain accepted. No words are generated at runtime. The progress indicator displays progress toward a 150-point milestone, not total dictionary exhaustion.

Note: Presence in the dictionary does not guarantee that a string is a common Persian word. Please report false positives and missing common words for further editorial review.
